# Trial 14: review

## Questions

How long does the person take to find what was inferred, and what is wrong, when the same content is:

1. one long document;
2. objects and commits, marked `from-me` or `inferred`?

## Setting

The same content in the two forms. [Trial 0](00-first-run.md) gives a real case: a long document already exists for its intents.

## Fixed before the trial

Not fixed yet. They are written here before the first run, and not changed after.

## Expected answer

None. We record what happens.

## Measures

From [measures](../../standard/measures.md): left to review, and the person's time.

## What could break

Fragmentation: small objects can read as badly as a long document. People read a story, and the views must recompose it.
