# Trial 24: elevation

## Questions

Two people, one used to structuring and one not, bring intents of the same nature over several weeks. For each of them:

1. How does the balance evolve?
2. How does anchoring evolve?
3. How do the Capital signals evolve (rephrasings, misplacements, corrections)?

## Setting

Two people, each in their own space with the Organization model ([organization](../../models/organization/model.md)), over several weeks.

## Fixed before the trial

Each person's consent. Their measures and signals are read with their agreement, and for them alone. The rest is not fixed yet; it is written here before the first run, and not changed after.

## Expected answer

None. We record what happens.

## Measures

From [measures](../../standard/measures.md): balance, anchoring, Capital signals.
