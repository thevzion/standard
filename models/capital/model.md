# The Capital model

> What can the person do now that they couldn't before?

Private, in the person's [home](../home/model.md). Not a separate domain: a reading of the loop,
fed by every space the person acts in, plus a few dated capabilities with their proof.

## Types

| Type | Question | Horizon | Fields | Serves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `capability` | What can I do now that I couldn't before? | dated | date, proof | nothing; private |

### Five branches, one per human verb of the loop

The branches are not objects written on the side: they are dimensions of the person, fed by each
loop.

| Branch | Verb | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Clarity | want | |
| Expression | say | |
| Discernment | see | at the center: you only choose well what you placed well |
| Judgment | choose | includes taste: what reduces rework |
| Learning | learn | |

The objects of the system are the objects of discernment: learning the standard is learning to
discern. The branches are not reserved to humans: intelligence sees, says and chooses too. Capital
is what the person can do themselves, and keeps when tools change; what only they can do is want,
and answer for it.

### Two capitals

"Both get it. Only one compounds." Two capitals rise together:

| Capital | What it is |
|---|---|
| **the source** | what the person said, kept and linked, which serves every next turn: an asset, in the ledger sense |
| **what the person can do** | see, say, judge, want clearly: the five branches |

"Reach isn't capital": reach comes from agents; capital stays with the person when tools change.

### Guardrail

Capital belongs to the person, and nobody else grades it. A grade on a person is never sold. The
branches are measured without targets.

### How the person learns

- **From the questions** (scaffolding). The forms of the explicit are cultural capital in Bourdieu's sense: knowledge passed on unequally, often tacit, applied without being able to say it (Polanyi: "we know more than we can tell"). A model writes them once; the agent applies them; the person learns them by answering the types' questions, seeing what was inferred, and correcting. This is scaffolding in the sense of Wood, Bruner and Ross (1976): a support that adapts, and is removed as the person learns.
- **From the environment**. The person lives in a space that already has the forms. Reading the sources, a projection, or the agent's replies, everything is already placed, typed, with its why; the person takes it in, as one learns a language by living it. This is situated learning, after Lave and Wenger (1991): one learns a trade by taking part in a community that practices it, first at the edge, then at the center. Here the community is the space itself, and the organizations it links to.
- **The opposite risk** is known: relying on the agent so much that one thinks less (a CHI 2025 study on over-reliance). The difference is what the person keeps: wanting, and answering the questions, not only receiving results.

In practice the agent files by default, marks what it inferred, and says why when it catches a
slip. The person learns by reading the catches, with no extra work. Candidate, tested by
[trial 24](../../tests/trials/24-elevation.md) and [trial 6](../../tests/trials/06-person-spots-catches.md).
